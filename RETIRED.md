# Juniper Auto Retirement Record

**Retired: September 5, 2026**

## Project

- Project: Juniper Auto
- Organization: Cinqic
- Final status: retired historical research archive
- Canonical archive: `Cinqic/Juniper-Auto`

Juniper Auto is no longer in active development, is no longer on Cinqic's
active roadmap, and has no planned continuation. This retirement is final; it
is not a pause, hiatus, delayed phase, or project awaiting additional
development.

## Final technical state

- Phase 0 was approved.
- Phase 1 was approved.
- Phase 2 was independently approved.
- Phase 3 produced a candidate tokenizer, but it remained pending independent
  review and was never approved as a completed phase.
- No Juniper Auto model was trained on a real pretraining corpus.
- No base checkpoint or instruction-tuned checkpoint exists.
- No production pretraining corpus exists.
- No autonomy runtime or finished tool system exists.
- No trained expert specialization was demonstrated.
- Later planned phases were never completed.

Incomplete work and negative results remain in the repository as evidence.
The Phase 0–3 reports, source, experiments, tokenizer artifacts, manifests,
tests, recovery material, and historical tags are preserved. Historical phase
documents are not rewritten to imply that the later retirement was known when
they were authored.

## Reason for retirement

Cinqic's research direction shifted toward a modular Juniper ecosystem in
which language models, tools, applications, retrieval, memory, agents, and
specialized components cooperate, rather than requiring one approximately
150M sparse MoE language model to become the entire system.

Future Cinqic language-model research will focus on models designed
specifically to work with Juniper App and the broader modular Juniper
ecosystem. No successor model name or specification is announced here.

Future models may reuse lessons from Juniper Auto's research, but they are not
continuations or releases of Juniper Auto.

## Preservation policy

This repository is retained as a read-only historical research archive. Its
Git history is not rewritten, historical tags are preserved, unfinished work
is not presented as completed, and research artifacts are not deleted merely
because the project is retired. The retirement tag `retired-2026-09-05`
identifies the final pre-archive `main` state.

The repository itself is the sole canonical location for substantive Juniper
Auto information after retirement. Cinqic's active repositories and website
do not maintain a product page, archive page, roadmap, dependency, or current
research presentation for Juniper Auto.
